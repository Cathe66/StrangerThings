## 📺 OVERVIEW

**3D PRO** is not just a display system; it is a digital rift. It allows users to interact with physical light and matter (pixels) using only their hands—no peripherals required. 

By merging **Three.js** high-performance rendering with **MediaPipe's** neural hand tracking, I’ve created a bridge between our world and the digital void.

---

## 🔦 CORE ANOMALIES (FEATURES)

### 🌀 1. Particle Atomization (Single Image Mode)
Upload a single image to see it disintegrated into **10,000,000 sentient particles**. 
* **Pinch Interaction:** Control the gravity of the rift. Close your fingers to bring the world together; open them to let it drift into the void.

### 🖼️ 2. The Direct Rift (Carousel Mode)
Point your index finger to the sky to activate the batch carousel. 
* **Direct Texture Projection:** High-fidelity imagery rendered directly on 3D planes.
* **Telekinetic Navigation:** Point **LEFT** or **RIGHT** to slide between realities.

### 🛡️ 3. Void-Proofing (Visual Tech)
* **Anti-Moire Shield:** A jitter-based sampling algorithm that prevents "interference patterns" from the other side.

## 🕹️ HOW TO OPERATE

1. **INITIALIZE**: Open `index.html` in a modern browser.
2. **SYNC**: Allow camera access (The Lab needs to see you).
3. **PARTICLE MODE**: Upload a single photo. Use a **PINCH** gesture to manipulate the rift.
4. **CAROUSEL MODE**: Upload multiple photos. Point your **INDEX FINGER UP** to start.
5. **TELEKINESIS**: Point **LEFT** or **RIGHT** while index is up to switch images.

## 📜 RESEARCH LOG (Description)

This project is a high-performance **Hybrid 3D Visualization Engine**. It explores the boundaries of **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)** by removing the friction of physical touch. 

The system uses GPU-accelerated shaders to handle millions of particles in real-time, while simultaneously processing a live CV (Computer Vision) feed to interpret user intent. It's built for those who want to feel like Eleven manipulating the world with a simple gesture.
